Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information HyperAccts collects and how it is used.

1. Information we collect

Purchase information: when you buy a license, our payment provider (Stripe) collects the information needed to process payment — name, email, billing address, and payment method. We receive an order reference, email, and license entitlement; we do not receive or store your full card number.

License activation: when you activate your license key, the desktop app may contact our licensing service with your key and an installation identifier to validate the license.

2. How we use information

We use purchase and activation information to fulfill your order, deliver your installer and license key, process refunds, and prevent abuse. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Workspace data stays on your device

Your campaign records, provider credentials, and workspace state are stored locally on your Windows device. Provider credentials are held in the app's local memory and are sent only to the provider you select for the operation you invoke.

4. Third-party services

We use Stripe for payment processing. Stripe's handling of your data is governed by its own Privacy Policy. The marketplace and any analytics are privacy-respecting and described on their respective pages.

5. Cookies

The marketing site may use strictly necessary cookies and, if enabled, privacy-respecting analytics. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you, as applicable law permits. Contact us through the channels listed on our site.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us through the channels listed on our site.
