A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A clear start.

What do I get for $1,499?

A perpetual single-seat license to the HyperAccts Windows workspace — every current workflow and every future one we ship, plus included minor and patch updates. On purchase you receive a Windows installer download, a unique license key, and an emailed receipt.

Is HyperAccts a Windows download?

Yes. HyperAccts is a downloadable Windows desktop app. The website is the public home for the product, the Everything license, and the future marketplace.

Is this a subscription?

No. The Everything license is a one-time, perpetual purchase. There are no tiers and no recurring fees to manage.

What happens in the preview?

The preview lets you create campaigns with mock data, simulate outcomes, organize provider connections, and explore demo solution unlocks before you buy. It does not create real platform accounts or process marketplace payments.

What is the refund policy?

14 days from purchase, void once the license key is activated in the desktop app. Full details appear in the published Refund Policy.
