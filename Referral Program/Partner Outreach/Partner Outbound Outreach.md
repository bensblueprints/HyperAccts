# Partner Outreach Program

## Overview

This document outlines the outreach strategy and templates for recruiting initial affiliates/partners for the HyperAccts Affiliate & Referral Program.

## Target Partner Segments

1. **Tech Content Creators** (YouTube/Twitch streamers, podcasters)
   - Focus on personal finance, SMB tech, accounting software reviewers
   - Target audience: 10K-100K subscribers/engine room listeners

2. **Open Source Maintainers**
   - Accounting-focused OSS projects (Ledger CLI alternatives, double-entry tools)
   - Technical communities (Hacker News, Reddit r/accounting, r/opensource)

3. **Accounting Communities**
   - CPA networks, bookkeeping forums, tax professionals' groups
   - Industry associations (AICPA, IRS outreach programs)

4. **Startup Accelerators**
   - Pre-seed/seed stage programs with accounting-focused portfolios
   - Partnerships for portfolio company license bundles

## Outreach Timeline

| Week | Activity | Target Count |
|------|----------|--------------|
| Week 1-2 | Lead identification, list building | 50-75 prospects |
| Week 3-4 | Initial outreach (email/DM) | 25-30 initial contacts |
| Week 5-6 | Follow-ups, first responses | 10-15 responses |
| Week 7-8 | Onboarding, agreements | 5-10 signed partners |

## Onboarding Terms for Initial Partners

### Auto-qualified Status
- All initial partners receive **Silver tier** status (bypasses 20-referral requirement)
- **15% commission uplift** on first 100 referrals (vs. standard 10% direct / 5% nested)
- **90-day conversion window** (vs. standard 60 days)

### Tier Benefits
| Tier | Base Requirements | Initial Partner Benefit |
|------|-------------------|------------------------|
| **Silver** | ≥20 successful referrals | Auto-qualified |
| **Gold** | ≥50 successful referrals | +White-label assets |
| **Platinum** | ≥100 successful referrals | +Revenue share options |

### Payout Terms
- **Monthly** on 15th (or next business day)
- **Minimum $100 threshold**
- **Methods**: Bank transfer, PayPal, crypto (BTC/ETH/USDC), HyperAccts credits (105% value)

## Outreach Templates

### 1. Initial Contact Email (Cold Outreach)

**Subject**: HyperAccts Affiliate Program - Partner Opportunity for {Prospect Name}

**Body**:
```
Hi {First Name},

I'm Alex, an engineer at HyperAccts. We've just launched an Affiliate & Referral Program to expand our distribution through strategic partnerships like yours.

HyperAccts provides accounting automation tools for SMBs, and we're looking to partner with creators and communities that align with our audience. Your work in {Prospect's niche} caught our attention.

Our program includes:
- 15% commission on our $1,499 license
- Tiered structure (Bronze through Platinum) with increasing benefits
- Tracking with 90-day cookie duration and last-click attribution
- Payouts: monthly, $100 minimum threshold, multiple methods

We're accepting 12-15 initial partners for Q4 2026 launch. Initial partners receive:
- Auto-qualified Silver tier status
- 15% commission uplift on first 100 referrals
- Extended 90-day conversion window

Would you be open to a brief call or email exchange to explore fit? I've included more details on the program in the attached spec deck and below.

Best regards,
Alex Morgan
Engineering Lead, HyperAccts

P.S. We've attached our Affiliate Program spec and would love your feedback before finalizing the Q4 launch terms.

🔗 Program details: https://hyperaccts.com/affiliates
```
