## Tracking & Attribution

### How It Works

1. **Referral Codes**
   - **Affiliate codes**: `AFF-[3-char-prefix]-[5-char-suffix]` (e.g., `AFF-MRG-2K9P3`)
   - **Referral codes**: `REF-[5-char-code]` (e.g., `REF-A7X2M`)

2. **Conversion Window**
   - **Standard**: 60 days from click to purchase
   - **Initial Partners**: 90 days

3. **Cookie Duration**
   - **90 days** for affiliate attribution
   - **24 hours** for direct referral attribution

### Attribution Model

- **Last-click attribution** (primary model)
- **Nested referrals** tracked to original source
- **Revenue attribution** to most recent touchpoint

## Payouts

### Schedule

- **Frequency**: Monthly (15th of each month, or next business day)
- **Minimum Threshold**: $100
- **Processing Time**: 3-5 business days

### Payout Methods

| Method | Details | Speed |
|--------|---------|-------|
| Bank Transfer | ACH/Wire (U.S. and international) | 3-5 days |
| PayPal | Instant or 1-2 days | 1-2 days |
| Crypto | BTC, ETH, USDC (ERC-20) | 1-3 days |
| HyperAccts Credits | Licenses with 105% value equivalence | Instant |

### Payout Ledger

Each customer purchase generates:
- **Revenue**: $1,499 license fee
- **Referral Fee**: Percentage based on tier and acquisition channel
- **Net to HyperAccts**: License fee minus referral fees
- **Partner Ledger Entry**: Transaction ID, referral code used, payout status
