# Partner Outreach Prioritization

## Overview

This document outlines the prioritization framework for the initial partner recruitment phase (Task #524: Recruit Initial Affiliates/Partners).

## Priority Framework

### Scoring Criteria

| Criteria | Weight | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|
| **Strategic Alignment** | 30% | Fit with HyperAccts ICP and product messaging |
| **Audience Size** | 25% | Reach potential (measured in subscribers/users) |
| **Technical Alignment** | 20% | Audience fit with HyperAccts technical capabilities |
| **Engagement Rate** | 15% | Historical engagement (comments, shares, replies) |
| **Ecosystem Complementarity** | 10% | Complementary to existing HyperAccts integrations |

### Priority Tiers

1. **Tier 1 (High Priority)**: Score ≥85%
   - 5-7 partners for immediate outreach (Week 1-2)
   - Expected conversion: ≥30%

2. **Tier 2 (Medium Priority)**: Score 65-84%
   - 7-10 partners for Week 3-4 outreach
   - Expected conversion: 20-30%

3. **Tier 3 (Low Priority)**: Score ≤65%
   - 10+ partners for Week 5-6 outreach
   - Expected conversion: 15-20%
